## EDM Wirecut machines (WC series)

Wire-cut EDM machines cut electrically conductive materials such as die steel (D2, H13),
tungsten carbide, copper and titanium using a thin travelling wire electrode. They are used
for press tool dies, punches, extrusion dies, gauges and precision components. Choose the
WC-400 for workpieces up to 400 x 300 mm travel; choose the WC-600 for larger plates.
The WC-400S adds closed-loop servo drives for tighter positional accuracy.

Installation requires a level floor, three-phase power with a stabiliser and a clean,
temperature-controlled room for best accuracy. Operator training is provided at installation.

## EDM Drill machines (DR series)

EDM drilling machines (hole poppers) drill small, deep holes in hardened steel and carbide
using a rotating tubular electrode. Typical uses are start holes for wire-cut jobs, cooling
holes and fuel-injection parts.

## EDM Sparking machines (SP series)

Die-sinking (sparking) EDM machines use a shaped graphite or copper electrode to produce mould
cavities, ribs and blind features. The SP-50 is available with a manual Z axis or a ZNC
controller for repeat jobs.
